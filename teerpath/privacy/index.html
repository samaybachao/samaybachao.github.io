TeerPath Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short

  • TeerPath has no sign-in or account. We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or GPS location.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's ads software collects some device data (described below) to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't receive any data that identifies you.

Who we are

TeerPath (Android package com.teerpath.app) is made by samaybachao, an independent developer. In this policy "we" means samaybachao. Contact: samaybachao.official@gmail.com.

Information saved on your phone

The app saves your game data on your device so you can continue where you left off: unlocked levels, best times, hints left, your Mind Sharpness score, daily challenge streak and the sound on/off setting. This data stays on your phone and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads, full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded ads. They are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. According to Google, the SDK automatically collects and shares the following data with Google:

DataWhat it is
IP addressYour device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location (city or region, not GPS).
App interactionsFor example app launches, taps and ad video views.
DiagnosticsPerformance information about the app and the SDK, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use.
Device identifiersYour Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this data for advertising (including, where allowed, personalized ads), measuring ads, analytics and fraud prevention. It is encrypted in transit (TLS). Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the app shows Google's consent message the first time it opens, where the law requires it. You can change your choice any time from Privacy options on the app's home screen.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off ad personalization, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Your game data: uninstall the app, or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps → TeerPath → Storage.

Children

TeerPath is a general puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or trade personal information. Apart from the ad data that Google's SDK collects as described above, the app does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

Keeping and deleting data

We don't keep any of your data on our own servers, because we don't have any. Game data stays on your phone until you delete it. For data collected by Google, see Google's policies linked above; you can also delete your advertising ID as described under Your choices.

Security

Game data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read. Ad data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Big changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
