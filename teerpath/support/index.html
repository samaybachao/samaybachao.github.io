How to play

Tap an arrow to slide it off the board in the direction its tip points. It can only leave if no other arrow is in its way, all the way to the edge. Tapping a blocked arrow costs a life; you have 3 per level. Clear every arrow to finish the level.

Hints and lives

  • You start with 3 free hints and get 1 more every 5 levels and for each daily challenge.
  • When you run out, you can watch an optional ad to get 2 more.
  • A hint makes one arrow glow and shows its way out with a dotted line.
  • Out of lives? Watch an optional ad for 1 more life, or restart the level.

Scores

Plan Strength starts at 100% each level and drops with every mistake or hint. Speed compares your time with a quick pace for that level. Mind Sharpness is a rolling score from your recent levels; harder worlds can score higher. They are game scores, not an IQ test.

Common questions

I reinstalled the app and my progress is gone.

Progress is saved only on your phone, so uninstalling or clearing app data removes it. There is no cloud backup yet.

Ads aren't loading.

Ads need an internet connection and can take a moment to load. If an ad can't load when you ask for hints, the app gives you the hints anyway.

How do I change my ad consent choice?

Open the app and tap Privacy options at the bottom of the home screen.

Your data

TeerPath has no account, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove your game data, uninstall the app or clear its data. To reset or delete your advertising ID, use Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
