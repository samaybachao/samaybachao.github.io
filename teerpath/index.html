TeerPath

Coming soon to Google Play

An arrow puzzle for your brain. Each arrow slides out the way its tip points, but only when nothing blocks its path. Find the right order and empty the board.

What's inside

  • 200 levels in 10 worlds, from easy to master
  • A different board shape every level: heart, star, ring, house and more
  • Arrows that bend into spirals, hooks and waves
  • A new look every 20 levels
  • Live timer, Plan Strength and a Mind Sharpness score to beat
  • Daily challenge, hints, sound effects

Free with ads

TeerPath is free to play and shows ads from Google AdMob. You can watch an optional ad to get extra hints or an extra life.
