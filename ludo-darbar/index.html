Ludo Darbar

Coming soon to Google Play

The royal game of Ludo, made bright and simple. Play against the computer or pass the phone around with friends and family. Works offline, in English and Hindi.

What's inside

  • Vs Computer with Easy, Medium and Hard players, or Pass & Play for 2 to 4 people on one phone
  • Three modes: Classic, Quick (first to bring 2 tokens home) and Master (capture before you can go home)
  • House rules you can switch on or off: open with 1 or 6, safe stars, extra roll after a capture, blockades, three sixes
  • Smooth 3D dice, token animations and sound effects
  • Daily reward, Lucky Spin, 12 achievements and a profile with your stats
  • Board themes and dice skins to unlock with coins
  • Your game is saved, so you can continue later
  • Colour-blind mode, fast moves, Hindi and English

Free with ads, coins are just for fun

Ludo Darbar is free and shows ads from Google AdMob between games. You can watch an optional video for extra coins or a spin. Coins are in-game points only: they can't be bought, sold, cashed out or exchanged for money or prizes. There is no betting of any kind.
