How to play

Tap your dice to roll. A token leaves the base on a 6 (or on a 1 too, if that house rule is on). Every 6 gives another roll, but a third 6 in a row is cancelled and your turn ends; the first two sixes still count. Land on an opponent to send it home and get a bonus roll. Stars and start squares are safe. Bring all four tokens into the centre to win. The full rules are in the app under Rules.

Game modes

  • Classic: first to bring all four tokens home wins.
  • Quick: first to bring two tokens home wins.
  • Master: your home lane stays locked until you capture an opponent. Until then your tokens go round the board again.

Coins and rewards

  • Win coins by finishing games, from the Daily Reward (7-day streak) and the Lucky Spin.
  • Achievements pay extra coins automatically.
  • Spend coins in the Shop on board themes and dice skins.
  • Coins are only for the game. They can't be bought or turned into money.

Common questions

Can I continue a game later?

Yes. The game is saved after every turn. Tap Continue game on the home screen.

I reinstalled the app and my coins are gone.

Everything is saved only on your phone, so uninstalling or clearing app data removes it. There is no cloud backup yet.

Is there online play?

Not yet. Online multiplayer is planned for a later version.

The video for free coins doesn't play.

Videos need an internet connection and are not always available. Try again a little later.

How do I change my ad consent choice?

Open the app and go to Settings → Privacy → Ad choices.

Your data

Ludo Darbar has no account, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove your game data, uninstall the app or clear its data. To reset or delete your advertising ID, use Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
