Rang Mela

Coming soon to Google Play

A color sort puzzle with the colors of a mela. Pour balls from tube to tube until every tube holds one color. Easy to start, and it gets harder the better you play.

What's inside

  • No login, no account. Open it and play, in Hindi or English
  • Levels that get steadily harder, with hidden-color mystery levels and a boss level every 10 levels
  • A live timer and a brain meter: the game adapts to how you play
  • Smart hints that show a real next move when you are stuck
  • A new theme every day, plus festival themes
  • Daily reward, sound effects and a share button to challenge friends

Free with ads

Rang Mela is free to play and shows ads from Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are always optional: you watch them only to get extra hints, an extra tube or double coins.
