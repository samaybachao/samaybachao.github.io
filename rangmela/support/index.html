Rang Mela Support

Need help or found a bug? Email samaybachao.official@gmail.com. Please mention your phone model, Android version and the level number.

How to play

Tap a tube, then tap another tube. The top balls move there if the other tube has room and the same top color (or is empty). Fill every tube with a single color. Win with fewer moves and less time for more stars.

Hints, +Tube and Undo

  • Hint shows a real next move that leads to the solution. You can watch an optional ad or use 30 coins.
  • +Tube adds one empty tube (once per level). Watch an optional ad or use 50 coins.
  • Undo takes back your last move and is free.

Common questions

I reinstalled the app and my progress is gone.

Progress is saved only on your phone, so uninstalling or clearing app data removes it. There is no cloud backup yet.

Ads aren't loading.

Ads need an internet connection and can take a moment to load. You can always use coins for hints instead.

How do I change my ad privacy choice?

Where the law requires a choice (for example EEA and UK), open Settings in the app and tap Ad privacy choices.

Your data

Rang Mela has no account, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove your game data, uninstall the app or clear its data. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
